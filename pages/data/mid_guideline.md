## Part 1. UNESCO Reading (15 points)

**Reading:** UNESCO *AI Competency Framework for Teachers* (2024), **Chapters 1–5**
(유네스코 교사 AI 역량 프레임워크, **1~5장**)

- **Multiple choice: 10 questions × 1 point = 10 points** (객관식 10문항, 5지선다, 문항당 1점)
  - Questions focus on the **main ideas and key concepts** of the reading, not small details.
    (세부 사항보다는 **핵심 개념과 주요 내용**을 묻습니다.)
- **Essay: 1 question = 5 points** (주관식 1문항, 5점)
  - The essay question will be **chosen from the practice questions** below.
    (주관식은 아래 **연습 문항 중에서 출제**됩니다.)
  - Write **one clear topic sentence** and support it with **two points** from the reading.
  - Your answer should be **about as long as the sample answers (4–6 sentences)**.
    (분량은 **샘플 답안 정도(4~6문장)**면 충분합니다.)
  - All answers should be written **in English**. (답안은 **영어로** 작성하세요.)

📄 **Study material:** [UNESCO Essay Questions](https://github.com/MK316/Digital-Literacy-Class/blob/main/pages/data/unesco_2024.md)
