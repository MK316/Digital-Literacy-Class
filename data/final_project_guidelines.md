## 📋 Final Project Guidelines

### 1. Select a lesson

Choose either Lesson 3 or Lesson 5 as your source material.

### 2. Choose two learning focuses

Select two complementary language learning areas and 
define clear learning objectives.

### 3. Develop a lesson plan

Plan the sequence of classroom activities, including how 
the teacher and learners will use the digital resources.

Your lesson plan should include:

- Target learners
- Lesson topic and duration
- Learning objectives
- Teacher and learner activities
- Digital resources
- Assessment

#### 📝 Sample Lesson Plan

| Item | Description |
|---|---|
| Target learners | Middle school students (Grade 1) |
| Lesson topic | Daily routines |
| Duration | 45 minutes |
| Learning Focus 1 | Vocabulary |
| Learning Focus 2 | Speaking |
| Learning objectives | Understand key vocabulary and describe daily routines in English. |

### 4. Build a multipage Streamlit app

Organize pages according to your lesson plan.

Integrate relevant learning activities, applications, 
and teaching materials so that both learning focuses 
are clearly represented.

### 5. Test and deploy

Check the following before deploying your app to Streamlit Cloud:

- Page navigation
- External links
- Learning activities
- Classroom usability

### 6. Create a video tour

Create a short video (3–5 minutes) in English to guide 
first-time visitors through your lesson website.

The purpose is to help visitors understand what the website 
offers, how to navigate it, and how to use it for English 
language teaching and learning.

Your video should:

- Introduce the lesson topic and learning objectives.
- Guide visitors through the main pages of the website.
- Demonstrate key learning activities and digital resources.
- Explain how teachers and learners can use the website in class.

**Requirements:**

- Record your screen while navigating the website.
- Provide spoken narration in English.
- Add a clearly visible link to the video on your Streamlit app.

Think of this video as a guided tour for someone visiting 
your website for the first time.

### 7. Present your project

Each group will have 25 minutes in total.

| Presentation Content | Time |
|---|---:|
| Lesson overview and learning objectives | 2 min |
| Overview of the website | 2 min |
| Learning Focus 1: Activity demonstration | 4 min |
| Learning Focus 2: Activity demonstration | 4 min |
| Design rationale and reflection | 3 min |
| **Group presentation** | **15 min** |
| Q&A and discussion | 10 min |
| **Total** | **25 min** |

#### 🎯 Presentation Guidelines

You do not need to demonstrate every app or digital 
resource on your website.

Select **one representative learning activity for each 
of your two learning focuses** (two activities in total).

For each activity, demonstrate:

- What students will learn
- What the teacher will do
- What the learners will do
- How digital resources will support learning
- How students' learning will be checked

Demonstrate each activity as it would take place in 
an actual classroom.

All group members should participate in the presentation.
